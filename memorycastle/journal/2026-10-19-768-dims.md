# 768 dims measured against 512: noise, 512 stays
Author: abhi+claude | Date: 2026-10-19
Files: src/embed/mod.rs, src/limits.rs

## Context
EmbeddingGemma 2 is 768 wide; `EMBED_DIMS` cuts to 512 (Matryoshka) for
1 KB per chunk. The user asked whether the full width would help. Nothing
had measured it.

## Decision
`MEMORYCASTLE_DIMS` picks the cut at space creation (128, 256, 512, 768;
a new space either way). 512 stays the default. Measured in a copy of the
store (`MEMORYCASTLE_HOME`), both projects reindexed at 768, both gold sets.

## Rejected
- Switching to 768: one gold question improved by one rank, one MRR went
  up 0.004, the other down 0.007, confidences moved by at most 0.02. Not a
  signal, and 768 costs 1.5 KB per chunk instead of 1 KB on every scan and
  on disk (75 MB against 50 MB at 50k chunks).
- fp32 weights: not run. 4x memory and slower int8-free kernels for a
  recall that is already 1.0 at top 3; nothing for it to fix.

## Measured
- Reindex 276 chunks at 768: 65 s (0.23 s per chunk, same as 512).
- AbhiGram gold, 44 questions: 512 recall@3 1.000, MRR 0.905; 768 1.000,
  0.909. memorycastle gold, 53: 512 0.981, 0.897; 768 1.000, 0.890. The
  recovered question is the reindex-time one whose answer sits in two notes.
- Vector scan at 276 chunks: 0.3 ms either way; query embed dominates.
